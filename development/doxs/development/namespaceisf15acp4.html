Hardware control signals for the SSD1331 SmartDisplay interface.

The driver uses abstract ACTIVE/INACTIVE levels so platform adapters can map polarity correctly (e.g. CS is active-low on the physical pin).

◆ SpiMode

enum class isf15acp4::SpiMode : uint8_t
strong

SPI mode required by NKK SmartDisplay devices.

Data is sampled on the rising edge of SCK (mode 0 or 3 depending on CPOL). Engineering kits commonly use CPOL=0, CPHA=0.
